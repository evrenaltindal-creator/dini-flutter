Prayer Path brings prayer times, the Qibla, the Holy Quran and a step-by-step prayer guide together in one calm, simple app. No ads, no account, no internet needed. Prayer times are calculated on your phone, and your location is never sent anywhere.

PRAYER TIMES
• Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, with a countdown to the next prayer
• Location from your device, or pick a city from the list
• Six calculation methods including Diyanet; Hanafi or standard Asr
• Prayer notifications with the phone's sound or a short adhan
• In Ramadan: imsakiye timetable, suhoor and iftar reminders, Tarawih and fasting pages

THE HOLY QURAN
• The 604-page Mushaf, turned page by page like a book
• 114 surahs and 30 juz; continue where you left off
• Verse-by-verse English translation by Marmaduke Pickthall (1930)
• Arabic text: the Uthmani script of the Tanzil project

PRAYER AND WUDU GUIDE
• Every prayer illustrated, in the order it is performed
• Guided prayer: leads you through the prayer step by step, rakat by rakat; the recitations are shown on screen and can be read aloud with the phone's voice
• Wudu guide

TRACKING AND CALENDAR
• Daily prayer tracker and missed-prayer (qada) tracker; records stay on your device
• Monthly calendar and a daily calendar leaf with the Hijri and Rumi dates
• Qibla compass (true north)
• Tasbih counter with targets and haptics

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: next prayer, live countdown, today's times; shortcuts to Tasbih, Qibla and the tracker
• Apple Watch: next prayer, countdown, today's times, tasbih on your wrist and a watch face complication

PRIVACY
Prayer Path collects no data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your location is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla.

Available in English, Turkish and Arabic.
