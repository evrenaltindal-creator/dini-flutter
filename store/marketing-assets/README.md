# Namaz Yolu App Store creative assets

These opaque, text-free brand images are used by the Namaz Yolu custom product
page in App Store Connect. They are marketing artwork, not application
screenshots, and can be reused for every supported locale.

The originals were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. The
creative direction was a serene premium emerald/navy scene with a sculptural
gold mosque, restrained geometric ornament, and an unlabeled compass motif.
The prompts explicitly excluded text, Arabic calligraphy, numbers, flags,
prices, fake UI, people, watermarks, and claims of religious authority.

The Turkish advertising poster is a separate lifestyle campaign asset. It
uses the real home-screen screenshot and app icon as references, shows one
relatable user peacefully using the app after prayer, and contains the exact
copy: `NAMAZ YOLU`, `Vakit, kıble ve ibadet rehberi tek yerde.`,
`İyi ki Namaz Yolu var.`, and `Şimdi indir`. It does not replace the
text-free Header or Search Results assets.

The Turkish Header and Search Results advertising candidates extend the same
approved lifestyle campaign. The Header uses `NAMAZ YOLU`,
`Vakit, kıble ve ibadet rehberi tek yerde.`, `İyi ki Namaz Yolu var.`, and
`Şimdi indir`. The compact Search Results creative uses `NAMAZ YOLU`,
`Huzurla takip et.`, and `Vakit • Kıble • İbadet`. Their uncropped generation
masters are stored beside the Apple-sized exports.

The App Store export uses a restrained blurred-background extension so that
the approved poster fits Apple's 1206 x 2622 px screenshot slot without
cropping its copy, person, phone, or call to action. The eight existing Turkish
store screenshots also have matching exports in `store/screenshots/tr/apple-medium/`.

App Store Connect upload completed on 2026-10-21. The custom product page has
one Turkish advertising Header asset, one Turkish advertising Search Results
asset, and nine iPhone screenshots: the advertising poster first, followed by
eight real product screens. The original text-free Header and Search Results
assets remain recoverable in Asset Library. Submission for Apple review is a
separate action and is intentionally not recorded as completed here.
